# Arabic Source Research

- Search with natural MSA phrasing, not literal translations. Use terms that
  surface institutional content: تقرير (report), إحصائيات (statistics),
  دراسة (study), وزارة (ministry), الأمم المتحدة (UN).
- High-trust Arabic source classes: national statistics bureaus, ministry
  portals (.gov.xx), UN Arabic-language publications, established outlets
  (Al Jazeera Arabic, Asharq Al-Awsat, regional dailies), university research
  centers.
- Watch for date ambiguity: Hijri vs Gregorian calendars. Always normalize
  to Gregorian and note the original if Hijri.
- When translating claims, keep proper nouns, program names, and technical
  terms in Arabic in parentheses on first mention.
- Dialect content (social media, local forums) is signal for sentiment, not
  for facts — never cite it for statistics.
